For nearly two decades, Identity and Access Management (IAM) was one of the most complex engineering challenges in cybersecurity. Organizations invested years building connectors, integrating applications, managing identities, and automating provisioning. With Generative AI, much of that engineering effort is rapidly shrinking—integrations that once took months can now be built in days. However, AI has not solved the real identity problem.

The next challenge is **Identity Intelligence**. Enterprises must continuously determine whether a human, machine, workload, API, or AI agent should be trusted at a specific moment. Static authentication is giving way to dynamic trust decisions based on identity, behavior, context, risk, intent, and runtime evidence. As AI agents and non-human identities begin to outnumber human users, traditional IAM architectures will no longer be sufficient.

The industry must evolve from **Identity & Access Management** to **Continuous Identity Trust**. Organizations should start investing in AI identity governance, machine and agent identity management, identity graph analytics, continuous authorization, and real-time identity observability. The next decade will not be defined by how efficiently we manage identities, but by how intelligently we establish, verify, and maintain trust in an autonomous digital ecosystem.
