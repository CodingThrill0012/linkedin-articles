*(Deadly serious about this)*

Being a CISO sometimes feels like you’re living in a wildlife documentary. Every morning, 100+ vendors emerge from the bushes… Eyes locked on you… Trying to convince you **YOU have a security problem**.

So yes — I hide. And that’s Step 1 of my **Anti-Overload Protocol™**. 😄

Here’s the rest:

**1. Security Meditation Mode (SMM)** Before I open email, I sit quietly and connect with **the universe of my assets**. I mentally scan logs, configs, IAM roles, cloud drift… I join dots… I ask myself: *“Where could something explode today?”* This 10-minute ritual saves me from 10 hours of firefighting.

**2. The 4-Bucket Inbox Detox** Strategic → mine Operational → team Informational → archive Noise → *delete like a ninja* My inbox is not my boss.

**3. Vendor Evasion Tactics** If a vendor can't explain their problem statement in 1 slide… …I “temporarily relocate to a safer timezone.” Only 3 vendors per quarter get past the firewall.

**4. The Golden Hour** 1 hour where no one speaks to me. Not even my shadow. This is where real CISO work happens — architecture, risk patterns, strategy.

**5. Day-End Brain Clean-up** 3 wins 3 worries 3 priorities for tomorrow

CISO brain = defragmented.
