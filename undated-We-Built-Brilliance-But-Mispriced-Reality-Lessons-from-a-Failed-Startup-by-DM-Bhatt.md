My story of Yet another failure, but some lessons to share- I was part of a startup that had everything going for it, a world-changing idea, a brilliant founder (not naming him as he is still out here trying new thing), and a product that truly solved a problem. What we didn’t have was economic gravity. We miscalculated it and it sank us.

Let me narrate the failure highlights  

**1)The Mirage of Infinite Scale -** We assumed cloud meant infinite scale = infinite opportunity. (Omg its not true for sure but it is true indeed)

**My Realization** -What it really meant was **infinitely compounding cost.** Every user we gained looked like growth on the dashboard but each one silently drained our runway. Our per-user cost was rising faster than our MRR. We didn’t measure cost per 1K tokens, we measured vanity metrics — daily actives, not daily spend.

 **2)GPU Costs: The Hidden Tax of Ambition -**Reality - (It still sucks) -We wanted the best models. Always the biggest, smartest, fastest. We didn’t think about GPU utilization or autoscaling ceilings. So when demand spiked, our AWS bill exploded. Autoscaling worked beautifully for performance, not for profitability.

That’s when I learned: “If your scaling policy doesn’t have a cost ceiling, you’re not scaling — you’re bleeding.” I wrote this in my earlier post too this is a dangerous situation to be in

**3)Pricing for Dreams, Not Math**

**Reality** -Our subscription model was emotional. We wanted adoption, not margins. So we underpriced — “Let’s make it affordable first.” The problem was, every plan we sold at scale increased our burn. We treated pricing like marketing, not like survival. We never asked: “Can this plan survive at 10× usage?” That question would’ve saved us months of chaos. Now the Cloud bill haunts me, my dream of being a Co-founder got shattered, my dream of building extraordinary product got shattered indeed!

** 4) The UX Trap** - We built beautifully. Every screen was elegant.

**Reality -** latency was random, retries doubled token costs, and users thought we were unstable. Every retry was money out the door. We didn’t track latency-to-cost correlation, by the time we did it was too late. **I take this as my failure fully as it was 100% my mistake as a new CTO  **

**5) Sustainability — The Unsexy Survival Skill**

**No its not Sad Reality but great Stupidity** - We thought sustainability was a “green” word. It’s not. It’s the core of survival in AI economics. Your subscription price must not just cover GPU cost, it must outlive the inflation of compute pricing, regulatory costs, and support, overhead. “A startup doesn’t die because it runs out of ideas. It dies because it runs out of arithmetic.” **Learn the Formula** = Add 10 % in the end for Inflation + 10% for Buffer +10 % for Zero Business state Operating Cost ( This is the minimum cost you would need to add post the Tech Cost, HR cost Taxes, Legal Cost, Compliance cost, Security Cost and other misc. cost)  

**I made a Promise to myself** If I could go back, I’d do one thing differently: open an Excel sheet: and make every dream pass through a formula. No emotion. No excitement. Just math.

Because innovation without cost discipline is still a liability.

**Innovation = Idea × Execution × Economics**

We had the first two and we failed, yet another failure added to my Hat The third one, we learned the hard way.
