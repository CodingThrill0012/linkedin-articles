*In Large enterprises its not uncommon to have world class Incident response teams, cutting edge technology tools, robust processes & frameworks & best of investigative minds. Yet they miss the Golden hour of response, why*

*Preparation without Practice is incomplete, effort without any assurance of desired result*

*In my journey of Incident Response Leadership, the learning says, having People, Process, Technology & even a framework is not enough, the big thing most organizations are missing is the Attack Simulation exercise*

*The Final Frontier - Cyber Emergency Drill*

*Even when you have all the above checks, if you have not stress tested your team, you are bound to fail, as the last thing you can do is practice it first time during the real crisis*

*Non Negotiable*

*1) Cyber War Room - Dedicated Command & control Center with Code Red, Blue, Purple protocol maps with for critical response*

*2) Cyber Response Plan - Clearly articulated plan with R&R, Rehearsed & Signed off at Executive level*

*3) SPOCs from All the Critical Functions - Tech, Legal, HR, Admin, Communication etc*

*4) CMDB / Network Diagrams/ Arch Diagrams - Already mapped assets to ensure you know how to trace, track, Contain & Finally take control of the situation*

*5 ) CYBER SWAT ( Trained on Cyber Emergency Response ) - Trained folks who are always ready & can jump in with full commitment*

* *

*In the AI ERA when its clearly human Vs Machines to win the battle humans must train hard if you don't sweat in peace you will bleed in battle for sure.*

### \#AI \#ERA \#Security \#CISO \#Leadership \#Learnings

*In the battle of human versus machines, victory lies in rigorous training. Stay vigilant, stay prepared.*
