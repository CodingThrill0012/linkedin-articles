I recently noticed a misaligned door lock. A few years ago, it worked perfectly. Today, it scrapes metal every time the door closes. The lock didn't fail overnight.

It drifted. Millimeter by millimeter. Cycle by cycle. Year by year.

That made me realize something:

This is exactly how AI security architectures fail.

Security engineers focus on attacks.

Mechanical engineers focus on failure.

They assume systems will experience stress, wear, misalignment, and entropy over time.

AI systems are no different.

Agents call agents. Models invoke tools. Identities delegate authority. Context changes continuously.

The biggest threat isn't always an attack.

It's drift.

Identity drift. Policy drift. Trust drift. Context drift.

The future of AI security is not just preventing compromise.

It's designing systems that remain aligned despite years of change, scale, and entropy.

**AI is no longer static software. It's becoming a dynamic machine.**
