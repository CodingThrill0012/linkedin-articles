**Starts from Ideation stage**

**Key Responsibilities:**

-   **Alignment with the Product Ideation Team on the overall expectations**

-   Ensure AI use cases align with **security and compliance policies**.

-   Assess potential **security risks** in AI applications, such as **data privacy, bias, and adversarial attacks**.

-   Collaborate with stakeholders to establish **security frameworks** for AI governance.

-   Conduct **threat modeling** to identify vulnerabilities early in development.

-   Establish a **risk management strategy** for AI-based solutions.

**2. Data Collection & Preparation**

**Key Responsibilities:**

-   Enforce **data governance policies** to ensure compliance with regulations like **GDPR, CCPA, and HIPAA**.

-   Implement **data encryption**, anonymization, and access control to prevent unauthorized use.

-   Monitor for **data poisoning attacks**, ensuring dataset integrity.

-   Establish **secure storage** and **data lifecycle management** policies.

-   Conduct **regular audits** of data sources and integrity.

**3. Model Development**

**Key Responsibilities:**

-   Assess AI model security risks, ensuring defense against **adversarial ML attacks**.

-   Implement **secure coding practices** and monitor **supply chain security** for third-party AI frameworks.

-   Enforce **role-based access control (RBAC)** for AI model access.

-   Develop policies for **AI model explainability and transparency**.

-   Ensure **bias mitigation strategies** are in place to prevent ethical concerns.

**4. Testing & Validation**

**Key Responsibilities:**

-   Conduct **security testing** for AI models, including **penetration testing and adversarial robustness assessments**.

-   Ensure **bias detection** and fairness testing in AI decision-making.

-   Validate AI models against **regulatory and ethical AI guidelines**.

-   Implement **incident response strategies** for AI-related security breaches.

-   Perform **comprehensive vulnerability assessments** before deployment.

**5. Corporate Induction & Change Management**

**Key Responsibilities:**

-   Develop **AI security awareness training** for employees and stakeholders.

-   Enforce **identity and access management (IAM)** for AI systems.

-   Ensure **legal and compliance approvals** before AI deployment.

-   Work with HR and legal teams to establish **AI ethics and governance frameworks**.

-   Establish a **communication strategy** for AI risk awareness within the enterprise.

**6. Deployment & Monitoring**

**Key Responsibilities:**

-   Oversee **secure deployment** of AI models with continuous security monitoring.

-   Implement **real-time threat detection** for AI systems.

-   Monitor for **model drift** and retraining needs to maintain integrity.

-   Establish **incident response plans** to mitigate AI security threats.

-   Maintain **audit logs and tracking mechanisms** for AI model behavior.

**7. Maintenance, Scaling & Updates**

**Key Responsibilities:**

-   Ensure periodic **security audits** of AI models.

-   Monitor AI scaling risks, ensuring **consistent security policies** across expanded deployments.

-   Oversee **patch management** and AI model updates.

-   Conduct **periodic compliance reviews** for AI-driven operations.

-   Develop **risk mitigation plans** for AI system scaling challenges.

**8. Decommissioning & Disposal**

**Key Responsibilities:**

-   Ensure **secure decommissioning** of AI models, including **data wiping and ethical disposal**.

-   Implement **secure model archiving** for compliance needs.

-   Conduct **final risk assessment** for potential residual vulnerabilities.

-   Maintain an **audit log of AI disposal activities**.

-   Establish a **framework for continuous AI lifecycle improvement**.

**Conclusion**

The **CISO plays a crucial role** in ensuring AI security, governance, and compliance throughout the **AI Product Lifecycle**. By integrating **robust security measures** at each stage, the organization can mitigate AI-related threats and maintain **trust, transparency, and compliance** in AI applications.
